What is it

What it means to wrap WETH

To wrap WETH is to send native ETH to the canonical WETH contract by calling its deposit() function. The contract keeps your ETH and mints the same amount of WETH to your wallet, one for one.

Why wrap

Native ETH is the network's own coin and does not follow the ERC-20 token standard. DEX pools, limit-order protocols, lending markets and NFT marketplace bids expect an ERC-20 token, so they take WETH rather than ETH.

Which contract

This interface works on Ethereum and on L2s that have a canonical WETH: Arbitrum, Optimism and Base. On each chain it calls that chain's own WETH contract, so check that your wallet is on the network you intend. Background: wrapped ether on ethereum.org.

ETH vs WETH

ETH vs WETH

The two hold the same value and convert 1:1, but smart contracts treat them differently.

Same value, different form

Wrapping does not change what you own. It changes the form so that contracts built for ERC-20 tokens can use it, and unwrapping reverses it at any time.

How to wrap

How to wrap ETH into WETH

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and select the network: Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism or Base.
  2. Choose wrap and enter how much ETH to convert, or use the max button, which leaves room for gas.
  3. Review the amount and the estimated gas. The WETH you receive equals the ETH you send.
  4. Sign the deposit() transaction in your wallet and wait for it to confirm.
  5. WETH appears in your wallet on that chain. Add the token if your wallet does not show it yet.

To go the other way, use unwrap, which burns WETH and returns ETH one for one.

Fees & gas

What wrapping costs

Gas only

Wrapping is a single on-chain transaction, so the only cost is network gas paid in ETH. There is no approval step, no slippage and no price impact because the rate is fixed at 1:1. See how gas works.

Gas on L2s

On Arbitrum, Optimism and Base the transaction is paid in ETH on that network, and the wallet shows the estimate before you sign. The amount varies with network conditions.

Keep ETH unwrapped for gas

WETH cannot pay for gas. Leave some ETH in the wallet for this wrap, for later approvals and for unwrapping, or you will not be able to sign.

Security

Staying safe when you wrap

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface prepares the call and never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Confirm the network and that the wallet prompt targets the WETH contract for that chain; lookalike tokens named WETH exist.
  • Wrapping needs no approval, so be wary of any prompt asking for a token allowance during a wrap.
  • Check the amount in the wallet matches what you entered.
  • WETH is a smart contract, and smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Wrap WETH FAQ

What does it mean to wrap WETH?

Wrapping sends native ETH to the WETH contract, which mints the same amount of WETH to your wallet. WETH is an ERC-20 token, so DEXs, lending markets and order protocols can use it.

Is wrapping ETH into WETH always 1:1?

Yes. The contract mints exactly as much WETH as the ETH you deposit, and unwrapping returns ETH at the same rate. There is no market price involved, so no slippage or price impact.

Do I need to approve anything before wrapping?

No. Wrapping calls deposit() with your ETH attached, so no token allowance is required. You pay only network gas for that one transaction.

Which networks can I wrap on?

Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Base, each using its own canonical WETH contract. Make sure your wallet is on the right network before you sign, because WETH on one chain is a separate balance from WETH on another.

Why keep some ETH unwrapped?

Gas is always paid in native ETH, never in WETH. Without a small ETH balance you cannot sign the wrap, a later approval or an unwrap.

Can I turn WETH back into ETH?

Yes, at any time. Unwrapping burns your WETH and returns the same amount of ETH, minus the gas for that transaction.
