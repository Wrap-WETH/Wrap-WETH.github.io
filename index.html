How wrapping ETH
into WETH works

WETH gives ETH the ERC-20 interface used by token-based applications. Deposit ETH into the WETH contract and receive an equal WETH balance, backed by the ETH held in that contract.

Unwrapping reverses the process: the contract removes WETH from your balance and releases the same amount of ETH. Your assets stay on the same network throughout.

questions, answered

Amounts, approvals, gas, and getting back to native ETH.

What is the difference between ETH and WETH?

ETH is Ethereum's native currency, used for gas and transfers. WETH is ETH held in a wrapper contract and represented as an ERC-20 token. Direct wrapping and unwrapping convert the token amount at 1:1.

How do I wrap ETH into WETH?

Open the app, select Wrap, and enter your ETH amount. Keep additional ETH for gas, check the Ethereum network and WETH contract in your wallet, then confirm the deposit. The contract credits the same amount of WETH to your address.

How do I unwrap WETH back to ETH?

Select Unwrap and enter the WETH amount. Confirm the withdrawal transaction in your wallet. The WETH9 contract removes that WETH balance and returns the equivalent ETH. Keep ETH in your wallet to pay the transaction fee before the withdrawal completes.

Is there slippage when wrapping ETH?

A direct WETH9 deposit or withdrawal uses a fixed 1:1 conversion and has no liquidity-pool slippage. Network gas is paid separately in ETH. Trading WETH through a liquidity pool follows that pool's market price and fees.

Do I need a token approval to wrap or unwrap?

A direct ETH deposit into WETH9 needs a payable transaction. A direct WETH9 withdrawal uses your own token balance. ERC-20 spending approvals apply when a separate contract, such as a router, spends WETH for you; check the spender and allowance before signing.

How long does wrapping WETH take?

Wrapping and unwrapping each take one on-chain transaction with the WETH9 contract. The balance updates when the transaction is included in a block. Inclusion time depends on network demand and your fee settings; finality follows later.

Can I use WETH on another network?

WETH exists on several networks, with network-specific contracts and balances. Wrapping on Ethereum keeps your assets on Ethereum. To move assets to another network, use a bridge route and check the destination token contract.

Does WETH earn staking rewards?

Wrapping changes ETH into an ERC-20 token. WETH has no built-in staking yield. Rewards from lending, liquidity provision, or staking come from a separate protocol and its own terms.
